# 14 Opposite --prefer answers diverge silently

`t113` · status **todo** · ready

Required by a specialist reviewing 0.7.0's status-conflict rule (2026-11-19), reproduced by the builder of the canonical status fix: two copies of an unkeyed store that each resolve one field conflict with --prefer ours end different, and both say already merged

Two copies of an unkeyed store each resolve the same field conflict with `--prefer ours` in the canonical merge. Each then reports "already merged" for the other's result, and `show` differs between them for good: nothing tells either copy they disagree. Older than 0.7.0 (the canonical merge's `--prefer` has always worked this way); not fixed by the 0.7.0 change that took status out of it.

Reproduction (dev build at the canonical status fix, scratch stores, 2026-11-19):

1. `init` copy A, `add "One"`; copy `.tasks/` to B.
2. A: `edit t1 -b alice`; B: `edit t1 -b bob`. Bring each copy's objects to the other.
3. A: `merge <B's HEAD> --prefer ours` (merged, 2 replayed); B: `merge <A's HEAD> --prefer ours` (merged, 1 replayed).
4. Bring objects both ways. A: `merge <B's new HEAD>` says "already merged: everything on ... is on this line"; B: `merge <A's new HEAD>` says the same.
5. `show t1`: A's body is "alice", B's is "bob".

Why: the merge treats the other HEAD as held once every original it names is on our line (`replayed_from`), so a resolution recorded only as which side's patch applied never reaches the other copy. The status rule's version of this (opposite `--prefer` on a status) was removed from the canonical merge by the same fix; field conflicts remain.
